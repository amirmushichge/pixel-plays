# Music

The `.mp3` files in the game folders are not covered by the MIT License. They were generated with [Suno](https://suno.com) for these games, and are included so the games play as intended. They are not licensed for reuse outside this project.

If you build on this code, replace them with your own tracks. A game runs without its track: only the background music is missing.

A game can also carry its own music as code. See [Synth music](README.md#synth-music) in the README. A synth song is part of the code, so the MIT License covers it.
